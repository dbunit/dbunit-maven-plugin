= Settings.xml

You can hide username/password in your settings.xml, ensure to configure "settingsKey" in your
pom's configuration, otherwise it will use your database's url as lookup key

[source,xml]
----
<settings>

  ....

  <servers>

    <server>
     <id>sensibleKey</id>
     <username>your-username</username>
     <password>your-password</password>
    </server>

   </servers>

 </settings>
----
